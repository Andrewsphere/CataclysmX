# City terrain and infrastructure artwork brief

**Status: Ready for artwork.** These stable IDs have terminal presentation; SDL
PNG artwork and mappings are separate work. Contexts below are intended generated
uses, not a claim that generation or content manifests already place these assets.
Keep palettes coherent across buildings and blocks rather than scattering colors.
Materials are visual identities, not extra material or machine mechanics.

Walls need connected edges and corners; preserve material continuity at floor
boundaries and entrances. Floors and paving should tile cleanly without obscuring
actors or items. Door art needs recognizable open and closed states and rotated
entrances. Glazing must visually communicate sight through it.
